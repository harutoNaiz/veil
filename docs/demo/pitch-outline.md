# Pitch outline (one section per slide)

Turning this into slides is a human task. Every number cites its report row.

## Slide 1. The problem
- Feeds show things you never asked to see. Blocking tools work per app or per keyword, not on what is on screen.

## Slide 2. How it works (the six helpers)
- Veil looks at the screen, finds what matches your words, and covers it. Six helpers do the work: see, find, match, track, cover, and learn from your corrections.
- Everything runs on the phone's AI chip.

## Slide 3. The privacy story
- Screen frames never leave the phone. Airplane mode still works.
- Test accounts only in the demo.
