# Demo script

Seven steps, in order. Times are targets in seconds (not claims). Backup video: `data/final/backup-demo.mp4` (PENDING-HUMAN: not yet recorded). Only cats, the fox and spiders are used. No explicit material.

## Step 1. The normal feed
- Say: "This is a normal Instagram feed on a test account. Nothing is hidden yet."
- Tap: open Instagram, scroll the test feed once.
- Expected: cats, the fox and other posts all visible.
- Time: 20 sec
- Fallback: cut to the backup video at 00:00.

## Step 2. Add "cats" in the Console
- Say: "I tell Veil what I don't want to see, in plain words."
- Tap: open Veil Console, Concepts, add "cats", Save.
- Expected: "cats" appears in the list with the Balanced mode label.
- Time: 25 sec
- Fallback: cut to the backup video at 00:20.

## Step 3. Cats vanish while scrolling
- Say: "Now I scroll. Posts with cats get covered before I can read them."
- Tap: switch to Instagram, scroll through the cat posts.
- Expected: each cat post is covered while moving; other posts stay clear.
- Time: 40 sec
- Fallback: cut to the backup video at 00:45.

## Step 4. Switch to Strict
- Say: "Strict mode covers earlier and wider. It costs more battery."
- Tap: Console, Mode, Strict.
- Expected: the mode label shows Strict; covers appear on borderline posts too.
- Time: 25 sec
- Fallback: cut to the backup video at 01:25.

## Step 5. "Not a cat" correction on the fox
- Say: "The fox got covered by mistake. I tell Veil it is not a cat."
- Tap: long-press the covered fox post, tap "Not a cat".
- Expected: the fox uncovers and stays uncovered on the next scroll.
- Time: 30 sec
- Fallback: cut to the backup video at 01:50.

## Step 6. Add the spider pack
- Say: "Packs are ready-made concept lists from the catalogue."
- Tap: Console, Catalogue, Spiders, Add.
- Expected: spider posts are covered from now on.
- Time: 35 sec
- Fallback: cut to the backup video at 02:20.

## Step 7. Airplane mode
- Say: "Airplane mode on. Everything still works, because nothing leaves the phone."
- Tap: pull down the shade, turn on airplane mode, return to the feed, scroll cat and spider posts.
- Expected: covers keep working with no network.
- Time: 30 sec
- Fallback: cut to the backup video at 02:55 (the airplane mode recording).

## Cut-scope variant
If the gate fails, run steps 1, 2, 3 and 7 with cats plus one pack (spiders), and use the backup video for anything unreliable. Never quote a number that failed AC-6.3-04.
